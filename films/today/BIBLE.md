# 把今天留一小块给我 · film bible

The song: Chan Meng, Taiwanese indie bedroom pop, ~78 BPM, 3:16, the vocal starts at 0.25 s with no intro. The film is one
ordinary stretch of days in an old Taipei lane, painted in transparent watercolour with a loose pen line: the same hand
as 独身主义的咖啡 (`D:\github_repository\solo-coffee-mv`, see its `films/solo-coffee/sheet.jpg`), but lighter, paler,
more daylit, more lo-fi.

## Three threads

1. **A small square of today.** The title asks for "一小块" of the day. The film's shape is the small square: the
   yellow sticky note on the fridge, the square of sunlight crawling across the kitchen floor, one lit window in a dark
   block, the small square tiles of an old kitchen and an old facade, the sealed lid of a soy milk cup, the strip of
   washi tape the lyric is written on. On every "留一小块给我" a pen line goes once round a small square in the frame.
   The unsaid words become blank yellow notes that float up into the sky.
2. **Mint against grey.** The rain, the lane and the night are grey-blue and violet dusk. The flat is warm paper,
   terracotta and pale tile. One fresh colour belongs to the mint on the sill; it grows a little every time we see
   it. The sticky-note yellow is the other.
3. **Two people, no faces.** Never draw a face. They are backs, hands, shadows, silhouettes in lit windows, two cups.
   - **我 (me, the singer):** dark-brown shoulder-length hair tied in a low loose ponytail, a cream knit cardigan
     (CREAM/SOY with OCHRE shade), a mint scarf (MINT/LEAF) outdoors at night, light-blue jeans. Hands: cream knit cuff.
   - **你 (you):** a little taller, short black hair, navy jacket (NAVY with INDIGO/PAYNE shade) over a pale grey
     tee. Hands: navy cuff.

## Palette (wc.py)

Added for this film: MINT, LEAF, NOTE (sticky yellow), TERRA, TILE, CELADON, SOY, OSMAN, DUSK, NAVY. The older pigments
remain (CREAM, OCHRE, AMBER, SIENNA, BURNT, UMBER, SEPIA, COFFEE, ROSE, SKIN, MOSS, TEAL, PRUSSIAN, INDIGO, RAIN, MIST,
PAYNE, SLATE, VIOLET, INK, INKBLUE). Daylight: leave paper for light, MIST/RAIN/CELADON shadows, TERRA and TILE for the
building. Night: PAYNE, INDIGO, DUSK, with warm window light left as lifted/untouched paper glazed with OCHRE.

## Sets and layers

Each set is painted on a sheet larger than the frame so the camera can drift and push. Opaque backgrounds are saved
`opaque=True`; everything else RGBA `crop=True` with an `anchor`. Coordinates the shots need go into the layer's
`meta` (manifest) and the module docstring.
